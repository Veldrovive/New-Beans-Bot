Have you heard of parakaryon? It was a single cell organism found off of the coast of Japan on an underwater thermal vent. When studied, they found characteristics of both prokaryotic and eukaryotic cells, therefore making it the only organism scientists have no idea where it sits on the tree of life.
They are also reasonably confident that it wasn't a result of parasitism by a bacteria or anything. Of course it could be a fluke, but could still be an actual thing.
